A Tradition of Intellectual & Moral Supremacy

Founded on the principles of academic rigor, character fortification, and global stewardship, The Kartik's Royalas International Academy stands as a beacon of world-class education for international scholars.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding admissions, scholarships, and campus safety.

What are the entrance examination requirements for IB Scholars?
Applicants undergo a cognitive assessment test, an English logic exam, and an interview with the Academic Dean to assess critical thinking and analytical capabilities.
Are merit scholarships available for exceptional students?
Yes, the Royal Founders Trust awards up to 100% full merit scholarships for students demonstrating exceptional prowess in STEM, Olympiads, music, or athletic performance.
How is student security maintained on the 150-acre campus?
Our campus is fortified with 24/7 AI-driven biometric security gates, perimeter sensors, registered medical staff, and professional security officers.
